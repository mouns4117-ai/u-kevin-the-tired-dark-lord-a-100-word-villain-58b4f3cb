A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired — an ember-keeper of three hundred years, counting souls by raven and ledger, who keeps losing the thread of his own eclipse speech. The tiredness lives in the pauses and the small deflections, never in a plain statement of weariness.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
